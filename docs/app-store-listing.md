## Description (4000)

Unravel is a quiet place to put your thoughts, check in with yourself, and take the day one breath at a time.

Thirty seconds is a real check-in. So is thirty minutes. Nothing here counts days or keeps score.

WAYS TO CHECK IN
• Write as long as you want
• Talk it out with a voice memo
• Bullet dump fragments instead of sentences
• Mood tracking with two sliders, no words needed
• Guided prompts when you don't know where to start
• Quick check-ins and gratitude notes
• A breathing exercise when you just need a minute

SEE YOUR PATTERNS
Your timeline shows every entry with your moods and feelings. Patterns gently points out what tends to lift or weigh on your days.

PRIVATE BUILT-IN SUGGESTIONS
Unravel offers a short, kind reflection, songs that fit your mood, and a hand-checked shelf of reading. These use rules and content bundled with the app. Journal entries and recordings are not sent to third-party AI, transcription, search or music-lookup services.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your journal is private to your account and never public
• Lock the app with a 4-digit code
• Export your whole journal as a text file
• Delete entries or your account at any time

MAKE IT YOURS
Eight colour themes with matching app icons, three font styles, and gentle reminders on the days you choose.

Unravel is for journaling and general wellbeing. It is not medical care, a diagnosis or an emergency service. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.

For ages 13 and up.
